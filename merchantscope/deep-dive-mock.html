Customer revenue concentration — testing the 80/20 assumption

The rule of thumb is that 20 accounts should carry roughly 80% of revenue. Ranked and plotted against ~340 active accounts, this book does not follow it — worth showing, since the honest answer to "is it concentrated" is not always yes.

Individual account share Cumulative share 80% reference

Top 20 accounts (of ~340 active) hold 53% of revenue, not 80% — this book is genuinely diversified on the customer side. The concentration risk sits on the supplier side instead: 58% of purchases with just 3 suppliers (§02).

Fenwick Construction (rank 1, 11% of revenue) holds 40% of the reported 90+ overdue balance — but £51k of that £85k is a contractual retention not yet due, leaving £34k in genuine arrears beyond terms.
